# Privacy Policy for BulletBox

**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

BulletBox (“the App”) is a mobile application for converting ammunition box yardage charts into scope click adjustments.

## Information We Process

- Photos you take or choose of ammo-box charts  
- Chart numbers you confirm or type  
- Scope settings and saved ammo boxes  

The App does not require an account or login.

## How We Use It

Chart reading (OCR) and calculations run on your device. Photos and saves are stored on your iPhone and are not uploaded to our servers.

## What We Don’t Do

We do not collect or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address for advertising or tracking. We do not use third-party analytics in the App.

If you email us, we will use your contact details only to respond.

## Permissions

The App may request Camera and Photo Library access so you can capture or select chart photos. Access is optional; you can type chart numbers instead.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Data stays on your device until you clear saved boxes in the App or delete the App.

## Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do.
