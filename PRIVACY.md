# Privacy Policy for BulletBox

Last updated: November 11, 2026

BulletBox (“the App”) is a mobile application for converting ammunition box yardage charts into scope click adjustments.

## Information We Process
- Photos you take or choose of ammo-box charts
- Chart numbers you confirm or type
- Scope settings and saved ammo boxes

## How We Use It
Chart reading (OCR) and calculations run on your device. Photos and saves are stored on your iPhone.

## What We Don’t Do
The Application does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information about you, such as your name, address, phone number or email address.

## Permissions
The App may request Camera and Photo Library access to capture or select chart photos.
